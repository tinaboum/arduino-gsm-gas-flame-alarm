  \sectiontitle{Project Overview}

  \begin{summarybox}
  \small
  Developed a breadboard safety prototype that monitors combustible-gas
  concentration and flame presence, presents the live gas value locally, and
  initiates layered responses when a hazard is detected. The system combines
  immediate on-site warning and ventilation with remote SMS notification so
  that its protective functions do not depend solely on network availability.
  \end{summarybox}

  \subhead{Engineering implementation}
  \small
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Integrated an Arduino Uno with an analog MQ-5 gas sensor and a
          digital IR flame-detection module.
    \item Programmed continuous gas acquisition, threshold evaluation, and a
          16$\times$2 I\textsuperscript{2}C LCD status interface.
    \item Implemented local alarm signalling through a buzzer and red/green
          status LEDs.
    \item Switched a 12~V ventilation fan through a relay while keeping the
          Arduino and GSM electronics on a separate 5~V supply.
    \item Interfaced a Neoway M660 GSM/GPRS module over UART and transmitted an
          SMS when the alarm condition was reached.
  \end{itemize}

{\scriptsize\bfseries\color{Teal}PHYSICAL PROTOTYPE VALIDATION}\par\vspace{2pt}
{\scriptsize
The LCD displayed stable ambient readings of approximately 15--26 raw ADC
counts. Controlled exposure to lighter gas increased the displayed value to
721, after which the alarm response and fan were demonstrated and the phone
received the message ``flame or gas detected.'' These values were not shown as
calibrated ppm measurements, and the video does not isolate a separate
flame-only validation sequence.}
\end{tcolorbox}
